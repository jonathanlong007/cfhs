Dense Archives & Multi-Era Timeline

Explore the sequential growth transitions from early Ohio frontier settlements to the boom of the canal era.

Before the arrival of the Ohio & Erie Canal, early pioneers established initial clearings along the Tuscarawas River. These decentralized farming settlements relied heavily on rudimentary wagon trails for transporting regional timber and agricultural surpluses Ref #01.

Archival Document Plot

The construction of Lock 4 transformed the community into a pivotal commerce node. The local dry docks quickly gained renown for building and servicing standard canal freight lines, cementing Canal Fulton as a bustling economic engine Ref #02.
