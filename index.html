A Tale of Three Villages

The pioneering spirits who resettled our portion of this river valley preferred the western bank of the Tuscarawas River. It was shortly thereafter, in the year 1814, that the village of Milan was established.

[Map Image: 1814 Plat of Milan]

In 1825, the State Legislature enabled the construction of our first interstate transportation web: the canals. Soon after, in 1826, the village of Fulton was drawn up exactly parallel to this new channel.

Once the St. Helena II canal boat operations got underway, the Heritage Society would continue its local historical coverage, officially adopting "The Canawler" newsletter format.
